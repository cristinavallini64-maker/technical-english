Exercises

Type your answers, then press Check. Wrong answers are marked in red: try again. The solution of a sentence is shown only after you have tried it at least twice.

1. Key words

Write the English word from the text (in orange) that corresponds to each Italian word.

2. Label the picture

Write the name of each numbered part of the news web page.

3. Vocabulary

Write the technical term that matches each definition.

4. Complete the sentences

Complete each sentence with one technical word. The first letter is given.

5. Rewrite the sentences

Rewrite each sentence starting with the words given, without changing its meaning.

6. Translation

Translate the sentences into English, then compare your translation with the model.

7. Summary

Explain what fake news is and describe how you can check if a piece of online information is reliable (100–150 words). Use the diagram you completed in the scenes, then compare your summary with the model.
