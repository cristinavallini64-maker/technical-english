Exam practice – Esame di Stato

Each prompt starts from a material, as in the exam interview. Prepare notes for five minutes, then speak for two or three minutes, linking the topic to other subjects.

  1. Material: a photo of a rooftop solar system with batteries. Describe what the picture shows and explain how the system works, from the cell to the socket. Link: electronics (semiconductors, inverters).
  2. Material: two 24-hour curves, solar production and household demand. Explain the mismatch between production and demand and the solutions available. Link: systems and automation (control, smart grids).
  3. Material: the Agenda 2030 Goal 7 icon. Explain why access to clean energy is a global issue. Link: civic education.
  4. Material: a photo of the Larderello geothermal plant. Explain how geothermal energy works and Italy's role in its history. Link: history (the second industrial revolution).
  5. Material: a photo of an offshore wind farm. Present the advantages and drawbacks of wind power and give your opinion on its future in Italy. Link: electrical systems (transmission to the grid).
